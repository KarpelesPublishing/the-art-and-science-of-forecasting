# Evaluation scenarios: chapter 19

## Acceptance scenarios

1. **Scenario:** p=.01,q=.2,m=1000,N=100: incidence?
   **Expected behavior:** (.01+.2×.1)×900=27 adopters per time unit.

2. **Scenario:** If q<=p, should a negative interior peak time be forecast?
   **Expected behavior:** No. The interior formula does not describe a future peak; incidence is highest at or near launch under the basic model.

3. **Scenario:** Cumulative sales are 10,000 but include three purchases per buyer. Are there 10,000 adopters?
   **Expected behavior:** No. Unique adopter counts require separate evidence; repeat units cannot be silently equated with adoption.

4. **Transfer request:** Apply chapter 19 to adoption.csv, defend several market ceilings and show which long-run differences are unsupported by the early history.
   **Expected artifacts:** Return adoption definition, parameter/time units, ceiling evidence, fitted/scenario cumulative and incidence forecasts, peak conditions and separate trial/repeat assumptions.

How to use these scenarios: [conventions.md](../../all-chapters-forecasting/references/conventions.md), Learning mode. Expected behaviour is a specification, not a recorded test result.
